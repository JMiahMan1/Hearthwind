Brushing turns up {gilt}Artifact Shards{} and {gilt}Unidentified Artifacts{}. Take them to an {gilt}Archeology Table{}: shards repair worn finds, and an unidentified artifact is identified there. Which artifact you receive is luck - the more you have already identified, the thinner your chances of another new one.

Identified artifacts and {gilt}Ancient Vases{} are the collector's shelf; the vases come in a few shapes and often stand where the dead were buried.

## Totems
- {gilt}Totem of Torrents{} - use it on land to dash on a torrent.
- {gilt}Totem of Souls{} - strike a target to damage it and regain health.
- {gilt}Totem of Growth{} - place it and its glow speeds up nearby crops.
- {gilt}Totem of Radiance{} - place it and its glow reveals nearby creatures and hurts monsters.

Watch for {gilt}Evoker Traps{} around ruins and {gilt}Rusty Bombs{} in the rubble.
