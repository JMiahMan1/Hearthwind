<|drop-cap@hearthwind:guide|letter=b,text=oiling water makes it safe. Put water bottles on a lit campfire and they come back purified.|>

Purified water never gives you Thirst.

;;;;;

{rubric}On the campfire{}

Use water bottles on a lit campfire. They boil for a short while, then pop back out as {gilt}purified water bottles{}.

;;;;;

{rubric}Cauldrons{}

A water cauldron with fire or a campfire beneath it is boiled: fill your flask from it and that water is {gilt}purified{}.

;;;;;

{rubric}Purified water buckets{}

Smelt a bucket of water to purify it.

<recipe;dehydration:purified_water_bucket>

Pour it out to make a pool you can drink from by hand without risk.
