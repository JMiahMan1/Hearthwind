Eight packs are sewn in these lands, from the little {gilt}Baby Backpack{} up to the {gilt}Endless Backpack{}: baby, frayed, plated, gilded, bejeweled, blazing, withered and endless. Equip one in your chest slot or carry it, then press `B` to open it, and an {gilt}Ender Pouch{} opens your ender chest the same way. Backpacks cannot hold other backpacks or shulker boxes, and crafting one into a bigger pack keeps everything inside it.

## Back and Belt
Sew two more slots into your inventory: one on your {gilt}back{} and one at your {gilt}belt{}. Press `G` to swap the item in your hand with the back slot, and `Shift+G` for the belt. Swords ride on your back, lanterns on your hip, and both show beside the hotbar.

## Trinket Slots
A column of trinket slots sits beside your inventory - hats and face, cape and necklace, belt, gloves and rings, shoes and aglets. Each slot only accepts what it is made for.
