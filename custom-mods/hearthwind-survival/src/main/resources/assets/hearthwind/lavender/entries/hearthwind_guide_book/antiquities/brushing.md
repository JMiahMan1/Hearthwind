Suspicious dirt and suspicious red sand hide something inside. Hold a brush and work the block until it gives up its secret - the {gilt}Iron Brush{} is the slow way, the {gilt}Diamond Brush{} is quicker and the {gilt}Netherite Brush{} quicker still, each use costing the brush a little durability. The brush sifts out {gilt}Artifact Shards{}, coins, bones and the odd treasure.

The humble suspicious sand of the desert is only the beginning: {gilt}fossiliferous dirt{} hides pieces of a {gilt}Villager{}, {gilt}Ocelot{}, {gilt}Wolf{}, {gilt}Sheep{}, {gilt}Chicken{}, {gilt}Creeper{} or {gilt}Guardian{} fossil. Set the skull and the body together as a display - some of them are said to scare off the very thing they are made from.
