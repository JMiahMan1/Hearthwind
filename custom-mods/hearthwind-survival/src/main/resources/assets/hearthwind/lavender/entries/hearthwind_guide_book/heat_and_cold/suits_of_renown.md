<|drop-cap@hearthwind:guide|letter=s,text=uits of renown are not merely plates. Each carries a trick of its own.|>

;;;;;

{rubric}The ten suits{}

- {gilt}Bone{}: skeletons take no notice of you, and the pieces wear far longer than iron.
- {gilt}Wither{}: your attackers may wither (a quarter of the time, for a heartbeat).
- {gilt}Warrior{}: up to 20% more damage the more wounded you are; 5% more with axes.
- {gilt}Heavy{}: no tricks - the heaviest protection in the pack, and it hangs on your shoulders like it.
- {gilt}Robe{}: 25% fire resistance, 20% magic resistance, and those who strike you catch fire.
- {gilt}Slime{}: cushions and bounces your falls; 20% explosion resistance.
- {gilt}Divine{}: once a minute, turns a blow entirely aside.
- {gilt}Prismarine{}: spiked - melee attackers are hurt by their own blows.
- {gilt}Wooden{}: turns aside a share of arrows and blunts blasts.
- {gilt}Steampunk{}: a radar helm, a clock on the chest, a double jump from the leggings, and steam-jetted boots that spare you falls; 10% explosion resistance.

;;;;;

{rubric}Finding them{}

All ten are crafted, and the recipes open as you gather their parts. Loot chests sometimes hold a piece, and the road's bullies sometimes wear them.

;;;;;

{rubric}Gilded Netherite{}

Take your netherite armour and a {gilt}Gilded Netherite Fragment{} to a smithing table. In fire, the full suit grants {gilt}Fire Resistance for a minute{}; it then recharges for a minute.
