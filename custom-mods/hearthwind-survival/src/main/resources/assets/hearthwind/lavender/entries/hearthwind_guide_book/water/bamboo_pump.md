<|drop-cap@hearthwind:guide|letter=a,text= bamboo pump draws clean water out of the air, one slow pump at a time.|>

;;;;;

Set the pump on any solid block and {gilt}right-click{} it once to attach. Hand it a bucket, a glass bottle or a leather flask, then work the handle: each use flips the spout out and the next use draws a pump, so the motion is extend, squeeze, extend, squeeze.

;;;;;

<recipe;dehydration:bamboo_pump>

;;;;;

{gilt}A bucket{} needs four pumps before it comes back {gilt}purified{}; a {gilt}glass bottle{} fills with purified water on the first; a {gilt}leather flask{} gains two units of water. Once a container is done the pump rests for a minute and tells you how long is left - {gilt}Pump Cooldown: 47s{} above the bar.

;;;;;



;;;;;

{gilt}Taking the container back{} is simple: sneak with an empty hand and the pump hands it over, ready to fill again. If {gilt}pump_requires_water{} is switched on, the pump refuses to work unless it can see water - the sky, a pool or a dripstone - somewhere in the fifty blocks above it.

;;;;;
