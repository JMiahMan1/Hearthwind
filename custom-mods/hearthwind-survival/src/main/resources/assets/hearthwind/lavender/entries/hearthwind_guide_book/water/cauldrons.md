<|drop-cap@hearthwind:guide|letter=a,text= cauldron hung over a campfire boils water for you while you work.|>

Build the campfire cauldron and set it on top of a lit campfire, then pour water in with a bottle, bowl, flask or bucket. It works through the water while the fire below stays lit and turns the lot {gilt}purified{}, ready to draw back out.

;;;;;

{rubric}A copper cauldron{}

The copper cauldron catches the sky. Rain fills it with {gilt}purified water{} and snow with powder snow; a dripstone stalactite above fills it with clean water too.
