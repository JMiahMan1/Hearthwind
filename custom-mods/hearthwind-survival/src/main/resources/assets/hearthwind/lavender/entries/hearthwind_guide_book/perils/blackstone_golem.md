{rubric}The altar{}

Build it in the Nether: set four {gilt}Chiseled Polished Blackstone Holders{} in a circle five blocks out from the centre, then a {gilt}Gilded Blackstone Shard{} into each. The altar raises itself, and the golem wakes.

{rubric}The fight{}

{gilt}600 health{} and a {gilt}14-damage{} swing. Below half health it flies into a rage and its blows grow worse.

;;;;;

{rubric}The spoils{}

- a {gilt}Blackstone Golem Heart{}: shift-use it (once) and a blackstone heart beats in your chest - {gilt}+1 attack{} and {gilt}+2 hearts{} while it lasts
- a {gilt}Gilded Netherite Fragment{}, the smithing upgrade for netherite armour
- blackstone (3-7), ancient debris (0-2) and netherite scrap (0-2)
