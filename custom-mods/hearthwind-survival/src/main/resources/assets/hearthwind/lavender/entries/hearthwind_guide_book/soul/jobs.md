<|drop-cap@hearthwind:guide|letter=a,text= job is a trade. Press J or open the third tab of your inventory\, and click a job to take it up.|>

You may hold {gilt}3 jobs{} at once. After joining or leaving, you must wait twenty minutes.

;;;;;

{rubric}The eight trades{}

Miner, Lumberjack, Farmer, Fisher, Warrior, Smither, Builder, Brewer.

;;;;;

The Smither and Brewer are closed until the Copper Age.

;;;;;

{rubric}Earning{}

;;;;;

A job earns from the work of its trade. A lumberjack earns from felled logs, a miner from ore, a farmer from crops and from the food you cook or craft out of them, a warrior from the slain, a fisher from every catch.

;;;;;



;;;;;

A smither earns at the anvil, the smithing table and the furnace. A brewer earns from each potion drawn out of a brewing stand. A builder earns from every block you set in place: planks, slabs, stairs, walls, fences, doors, stone bricks and obsidian.

;;;;;

Rarer work pays more, and a strong brew pays more than a plain one. Each new level may bring a reward.

;;;;;

{faded}Every level costs more than the last, so the highest trades are long roads.{}

{faded}You can also use /job join, /job leave and /job info.{}
