{rubric}Raising a fallen friend{}

;;;;;

Crouch and right-click your fallen friend with an empty hand. Their {gilt}Revive{}/ button on the screen lights up, and one click brings them back - at 1 heart, which is enough to run, and no more. They rise with a lingering {gilt}Aftermath{} debuff for half a minute.

;;;;;

{faded}Another blow while downed is the end. Alone, there is no one to lift you, so you stay down.{}
