# Getting Started

Welcome to Hearthwind. Survive the first cold night, learn the skills, and build toward the next Age.

- Gather loose surface rock and flint.
- Keep warm, hydrated, and fed.
- Open your inventory with E, Skills with K, and Jobs with J.
- Use this guidebook whenever you need a reminder.
